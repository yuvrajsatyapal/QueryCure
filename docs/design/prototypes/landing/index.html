01 · The plan

A plan shows what ran, not why.

Seq scans, sorts and misestimates are symptoms. Finding the cause means reading the plan against the schema, the indexes and the statistics together.

02 · The advice

Suggestions are cheap. Measurements aren't.

An index that looks right can do nothing, slow down writes, or change results. It has to be tested before anyone calls it a fix.

03 · The proof

One fast run isn't evidence.

Noise, warm caches and small samples can make a change look faster than it is. A claim needs repeated measurements and a stated uncertainty.
